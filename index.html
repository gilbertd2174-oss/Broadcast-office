How posting works

X, Threads, Bluesky, LinkedIn, Reddit and WhatsApp open with your text already filled in. Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube don't allow that from a web page, so the text is copied and you paste it.

Post to selected sites opens the first site that still needs a post. Come back, tap it again for the next one.

Photos and videos: the quickest way is Post directly below, which uploads them to every site for you (Windows app). Otherwise, after a site opens, add them in its post box: tap Copy on a photo and press Ctrl+V, or tap Save and choose the saved file.

Post directly

Windows app

Upload your photo or video and post it to every selected site in one step, with no copying or saving. This uses a free Upload-Post account (10 uploads a month free) and works in the Broadcast Office Windows app.

  1. Sign up free at app.upload-post.com.
  2. Open Manage Users, create a profile (for example broadcast) and connect your TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and other accounts to it.
  3. Open API Keys, generate a key, and paste it below with your profile name.

Your key is stored encrypted on this computer and is only sent to Upload-Post.

Scheduled posts

Posts waiting for their time. When one is due, tap Post now. It loads the text, photos and sites into the Post tab so you can send it.

Broadcast Office can't publish while it's closed. For posts that go out by themselves, schedule them inside Buffer or Business Suite instead.
